# Privacy Policy — Japa Meditation (Android)

_Last updated: October 25, 2026_

Japa Meditation is a free app made by Vraja Das. It has no ads, no accounts and no analytics.

**Data stored.** Your session history (mantra count, duration, time and any notes you type) is saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it. The developer never receives this data.

**Microphone.** The app asks for microphone access only when you turn on *Auto Detection*. Audio goes to your phone's built-in speech recognition service (usually Google's) so the mantra words can be recognised. This app does not record, store or share the audio. The speech service's own privacy policy covers how it handles audio. For Google, see https://policies.google.com/privacy.

**Internet.** The app itself makes no network requests. The system speech service may use the internet to do recognition.

**Children.** The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

**Contact.** Questions? Open an issue at https://github.com/vraja-pro/japa-meditation-android/issues.
